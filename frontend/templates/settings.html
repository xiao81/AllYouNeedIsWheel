Connection checklist

  1. Open your broker app

    Log in to Trader Workstation or IB Gateway. Choose the same Paper or Live account you select here.

  2. Enable API access

    In TWS, open Global Configuration → API → Settings and enable socket clients. Check the socket port matches this page.

  3. Save, then test

    A successful test lists the accounts available in your broker session. It never submits an order.

Read-only mode lets you explore your portfolio and options without sending orders.
